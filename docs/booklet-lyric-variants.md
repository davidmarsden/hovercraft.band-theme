# Booklet note — lyric variants

The release booklet should include a short note explaining that Hovercraft lyrics survive in multiple imperfect sources — recordings, handwritten sheets, later artwork/transcriptions and band memories — and that Piers sometimes rewrote lines in performance.

A small selection of genuinely disputed or memorable readings can be preserved there as part of the band's history, rather than forcing every ambiguity into the website's canonical lyric text.

Candidate examples already discussed include the competing remembered/heard readings in `Angel` and `Killer Blues`, and the `Oh Yeah` “lied to” / “relied…” line. These should be checked against the surviving recordings and source sheets when the booklet is assembled, not automatically resolved in favour of grammatical neatness.
