# Archive + Story review checklist

- `/archive/` renders existing posts grouped by year and preserves original post permalinks.
- `/story/` renders the bespoke four-chapter foundation instead of generic page prose.
- Homepage shows one `From the archive` doorway rather than three generic latest-post cards.
- Existing `/music/`, `/lyrics/`, `/press/` and ordinary pages retain their current templates.
- Mobile layouts collapse archive/story grids cleanly.
- No post content or permanent post URL is migrated or deleted in this PR.
