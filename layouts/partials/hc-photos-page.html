The evidence

Not a gallery.
A box of photographs.

Hovercraft existed before everybody carried a camera. What survives is uneven: rehearsal rooms, live shots, scans, rediscovered prints and newer photographs from the return.

The archive is curated rather than scraped from every post: people, live photographs, press cuttings, drawings, handwritten song sheets, set lists and the other debris that survived.

Original photography: Abby Rushton photographed Hovercraft live at The County, Grimsby; Kev Kenning took the promotional photographs at his home in Goole. Photographs from these sessions were later used directly or adapted as source material for album and track artwork.
