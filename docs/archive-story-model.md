# Story + Transmission Archive

The public site now separates four jobs that had started to overlap:

- **Music** — listen to the records.
- **Songbook** (`/lyrics/`) — song-by-song recordings, versions, lyrics and provenance.
- **Story** (`/story/`) — the retrospective narrative of Hovercraft: 1995/96, the long gap, the 2025 return and the 2026 records.
- **Transmission Archive** (`/archive/`) — the contemporary record: original dated posts, release dispatches, reviews and outside reactions as they happened.

Editorial rule: **Story is memory; Archive is evidence.** Do not duplicate Songbook archaeology into Archive.

## Homepage

The former three-card Latest/Transmission feed is intentionally reduced to a single archive doorway. The chronological posts remain valuable, but no longer compete with the release-led homepage.
