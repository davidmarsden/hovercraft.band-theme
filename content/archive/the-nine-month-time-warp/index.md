**Hovercraft mythology · by Ron Nasty**

*A reconstruction of actual events, thirty years later.*

**Pepper:** I didn’t realise it was only nine months. I thought it was a couple of years 🤣 Must have been because we were awake so much of the time.

**Shimble:** I’d agree. Seemed much longer.

**God:** It was only about four months with Dan as our drummer!

**Shimble:** I’d have to check my receipts. I remember buying the drum kit with the army payout I got, when the young fella was drumming, and then starting to play with you guys very shortly after that. I remember you all recorded Tooting Brown and then the young fella’s parents didn’t like him hanging out with reprobates. Pretty sure it was a year or so, ’cos I used to visit Lady Penelope and her little girl had at least one birthday during that time. I think there’s a chance Gol’s memory has removed a chunk of time due to drug-taking and mental health relapses. 🤷 But I also can’t claim great memory, as it seemed like one very long day 😎 I know we lived in the house for a couple of years, didn’t we?

**God:** I’m only repeating what Ron told me. I can’t remember anything!

**Shimble:** Well, you can’t trust that fucker. Me, Belvoir-Hunter, and Thomo went to Holland in early April 1997, when we stopped living at the Vicar’s house. I think I was staying at Boozy Bill’s house then, though I wasn’t there long—I recall being there at Christmas time doing smack and amphetamines. Don’t remember when we moved to the Vicar’s after leaving East Ravendale, but I do remember the advert for finding Pepper being drawn up quite soon after we moved in, and then, hey presto, Pepper showed up and the band began to form.

*God produces the [original, date-marked 1995/96 press release](/press/#press-original-1996) introducing the band and its members.*

**Shimble:** *(after seeing the press release)* Wow.

**Pepper:** Brings back memories. Or a memory, at least.

**God:** Yeah, that’s all true. We moved to the Vicar’s place in summer ’95, I think. Pepper turned up maybe in September. We recorded Tooting Brown at Pete’s Pad in October. Piers took a long Xmas holiday and we restarted in February ’96. Jazz Hands played drums at the first Ropewalk gig. You got your kit and played all the gigs afterwards, IIRC. We were done by July.

**Pepper:** Wow.

**Shimble:** Well, that’s remarkable. I’m with Pepper—it seemed so much longer. But on reflection, my drumming was pretty fucking good for the small amount of time I was banging on them. Seems like I’d put in years of training in a matter of weeks.

**Pepper:** Yeah, you were the best.

**Shimble:** Could’ve been real good by now.

**Pepper:** The others were fuck-ups. Not that I remember Jazz Hands.

**Shimble:** Jazz Hands was a bit of a knob.

**Shimble:** *(in reference to “fuck-ups”)* As opposed to drug addicts.

**Pepper:** As opposed to consummate professionals.

**Nasty:** He was a clean-living boy.

**God:** He was all right, but he was a jazz drummer and just doing us a favour.

**Shimble:** Yep. A knob.

**Pepper:** Bloody jazz drummers with their paradiddles.

**Shimble:** He only drummed ’cos I had to drive cars for Jim’s brother for fuck-all cash. Stealing my drummer stool position for jazz kicks. No morals.

**Pepper:** I was in a band for a few weeks in Bournemouth when I first got here. The drummer was a bald French bloke we called The Gnome, who had mental issues of some sort. He could do spectacular flourishes on the drums, but couldn’t keep time. One time he actually left the stage during the middle of a song to go to the toilet.

**Shimble:** Europeans, eh.

**Pepper:** Right.

**Nasty:** The Gnome, The Gnome, the shit metronome.

**Shimble:** See you all at band practice 🥁

**Nasty:** I’ve got it pencilled in for December 2056.

**Shimble:** I can likely make that appointment.

**Belvoir-Hunter:** Jesus, nine months—just like the length of pregnancy for birth. Although I struggle to remember the day of conception!

**Shimble:** Hasn’t that always been the case for you?

**Belvoir-Hunter:** I’m sure Pepper is the father, mate.

**Shimble:** Perhaps the godfather.
