# Release-day store checklist

1. Create the album product in Payhip and copy its direct product URL.
2. Add that URL as `store_url` on the matching album in `data/releases.json`.
3. Add only confirmed live streaming URLs under the album's `streaming` object.
4. Keep 2025 and 2026 recordings as separate release records even when titles overlap.
5. Check `/store/` and `/music/` at phone, tablet and desktop widths.
