# Lyric editorial policy

Hovercraft lyrics survive through recordings, handwritten sheets, artwork, later transcriptions and band members' memories. These sources do not always agree, and Piers also changed words between performances.

For the website and release archive:

- normalise punctuation, capitalisation and minor orthography where useful;
- do not silently replace an audibly sung word merely because another reading is more grammatical;
- where a lyric is genuinely ambiguous, weigh the recording, original sheets/artwork and contemporary memory together rather than treating any one source as infallible;
- treat documented performance variants as variants, not errors;
- preserve notable contested readings for the release booklet/editorial notes rather than cluttering the public lyric pages with every disputed syllable.

The digital booklet is the preferred home for the entertaining and genuinely useful textual archaeology around disputed readings.
