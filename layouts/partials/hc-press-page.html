The short bio

Grimsby. 1995.
Gone. Back.

Hovercraft formed in Grimsby in the mid-1990s around songs written by Piers “Charlie Pepper” Wildman and played by David Marsden and friends. The band left behind tapes, songs and unfinished business rather than a conventional career.
