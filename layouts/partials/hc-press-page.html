The short bio

Grimsby. 1995.
Gone. Back.

Hovercraft formed in Grimsby in the mid-1990s around songs written by Piers “Charlie Pepper” Wildman and played by David “Golly God” Marsden, Aaron “Ron Nasty” Downing, and Daniel “Mr Shimble” Downing. The band left behind tapes, songs and unfinished business rather than a conventional career.

Original cuttings / 1996

Before the comeback.

Contemporary documents record the band’s first run, including the six-song live demo at Sounds Good and a local demo chart that placed Mr Tooting Brown at number one and New Pine Overcoat at number seven.

June 1996 press release

The band’s own account of its October 1995 formation, early gigs and forthcoming appearances. The advertised July and August dates did not take place: Spanish Steps, 22 June, was the last show.

Introducing Hovercraft

An early band biography naming the original members and March 1996 arrival of Mr Shimble. Its five-song description of From The Cradle differs from the six-song recording; some copies may have omitted Take It Or Leave It.

Steve Jackson’s South Bank Show

The local newspaper clipping discusses the recordings at Chris McRae’s Sounds Good studio and the band’s live following.

Demo Top Ten

A local chart with two Hovercraft entries: Mr Tooting Brown at number one and New Pine Overcoat at number seven.

Original scans are part of the band’s photographic and press archive. The handwritten management agreement names Shaun K. Stokes — Sean Belvoir-Hunter’s band alias — as manager; the surviving copy is unsigned.
