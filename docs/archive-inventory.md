# Initial transmission inventory

The first archive pass deliberately uses the site's existing Micro.blog `post` collection rather than copying review material into theme data.

Known high-value 2025 material includes:

- release announcements and first-person song/release notes;
- the `Mr Tooting Brown` history post;
- contemporary reviews of *Shaken Not Stirred*;
- contemporary reviews of *On The Rocks*;
- coverage of `Mr Tooting Brown`, `Anytime` and `The Promised Land`;
- interviews and outside reactions already collected on `/press/`.

The archive template groups all original posts by year and preserves their existing permalinks. A later content pass should classify posts (Hovercraft dispatch / review / interview / release) from explicit front matter or a maintained data register, not brittle title matching.
